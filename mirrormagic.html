MirrorMagic Free VST Plugins

Fingertip Control of PC From Android Tablet

MirrorMagic puts your Windows PC's screen on your tablet and lets you control it by touch. Tap to click, slide to drag, pinch to zoom in on small buttons, and type with the tablet's keyboard. It runs over your own Wi-Fi, with no account to create and nothing to install on the tablet. Open the tablet's browser, enter the PIN shown on your PC, and you're in

It was built for the one-person studio. If you record alone, you know the problem: your DAW is on the computer, and you're across the room at the drums, the keyboard, or the microphone. Every take means walking back to the desk. With MirrorMagic, a tablet on a stand beside you becomes your DAW's screen. Start recording, stop, undo a take, move a fader, or open a plugin without leaving your instrument. Because MirrorMagic mirrors the screen itself, it works with any DAW, and with any other program on your PC

PC programs are made for a mouse, so MirrorMagic is made for fingers. Zoom in up to six times, move around with a small map of your whole screen, and save your favorite close-ups so one tap takes you straight to your mixer or your transport controls
