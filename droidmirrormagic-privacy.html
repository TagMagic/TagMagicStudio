DroidMirrorMagic Privacy Policy

                                                                                                                                                                                                                       
October 26, 2026

DroidMirrorMagic is made by TagMagic Studio. This policy explains what information the app uses and how it is handled.

What stays on your computer
DroidMirrorMagic works entirely between your phone and your PC on your own network or USB cable. Your phone’s screen, sound, files, messages and everything else you see in the mirror window are never sent to TagMagic Studio or anyone else.

The app saves a few things on your own PC so it can work: your settings, the names and Wi-Fi addresses of the phones you’ve connected, your trial start date, your license key, and a log file for troubleshooting. These stay on your computer and are never uploaded.

What we don’t collect
DroidMirrorMagic has no ads, no tracking and no analytics. It does not collect personal information, usage data or your location.

Purchases
When you click Buy, your web browser opens our checkout page, which is run by Stripe. Stripe handles your payment information under its own privacy policy (stripe.com/privacy). TagMagic Studio never sees your card number. We receive your name and email address so we can send your license key.

Your email address
We use your email address only to send your license key and to answer your support questions. We never sell or share it.

Support
If you email us for help and choose to attach your log file, we use it only to fix your problem.

Third-party software
DroidMirrorMagic includes the open-source tools scrcpy and Android Debug Bridge (adb), which run only on your computer and phone and send nothing to outside servers.

Children
DroidMirrorMagic is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes
If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
