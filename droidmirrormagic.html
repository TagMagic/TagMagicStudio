DroidMirrorMagic puts your Android phone right on your Windows PC screen, where you can use it with your mouse and keyboard. Answer messages, scroll through apps, type with a real keyboard and drag files over, all without picking up your phone

Other mirroring apps make you fight to connect: command prompts, errors that aren’t really errors, and reconnecting every time you leave the house. DroidMirrorMagic was built to end that. Plug your phone in once, and from then on it connects by itself over Wi-Fi whenever you come home. No buttons to press and nothing to type
