How It Works

  1. Pick a topic: a TV show, your city, the county, the state, and so on.
  2. Name it: the show, the company, the bill number, or the issue.
  3. Pick the kind of message and type what you have to say.
  4. Press Find the Right Places.
  5. For each place: press Copy Message, then Open. Paste it in and send it there. Email places open a new email with your message already in it.
  6. Press Mark Sent so it goes in your Sent Log.

Check "I have an account" once you've signed up somewhere, so you'll know next time.

Badges tell you how much weight a place carries. "Official record" places become part of the public record that decision-makers have to receive.
