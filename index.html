Messy song titles. Missing artist names. Wrong track numbers or album names. TagMagic Studio scans your music files, figures out what each song actually is, and fixes the metadata automatically — song title, artist, album, and track number — for your entire folder in one pass, instead of fixing each song by hand one at a time
