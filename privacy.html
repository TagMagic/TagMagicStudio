Privacy Policy

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  9/16/2026


This policy explains what information TagMagic (the desktop application) and TagMagicStudio.com (this website) collect, and how that information is used.


The TagMagic Application

TagMagic is a desktop program that runs on your own computer. It does not collect, store, or transmit your personal information to us.

What TagMagic sends over the internet: When you use TagMagic's metadata correction or album/artist art features, the song, artist, and album names you're looking up are sent to third-party music databases in order to find matching information. These services include:

MusicBrainz / Cover Art Archive
iTunes Search API (Apple)
Discogs
Deezer
TheAudioDB
fanart.tv
Wikipedia

These lookups only include the text needed to identify a song or album (such as artist and album name) — never your personal information, your file paths, or any other content from your computer. Each of these third-party services has its own privacy policy governing how they handle requests sent to them.

What TagMagic stores locally: If you purchase a license, TagMagic saves your email address and license key in a small file on your own computer (used only to confirm your copy is licensed). This file stays on your computer and is never sent to us or anyone else.


This Website (TagMagicStudio.com)

This site is hosted using Cloudflare. We do not use separate analytics or tracking tools (such as Google Analytics) beyond what Cloudflare provides as part of standard web hosting and security (for example, basic traffic logs used to keep the site running and secure).

Downloads: The TagMagic installer is hosted via Cloudflare R2 storage. Downloading the file does not require you to provide any personal information.

Purchases: If you purchase a TagMagic license, payment is processed securely by Stripe, a third-party payment processor. We do not see or store your full payment card details — Stripe handles that directly. As part of your purchase, we receive your email address so that we can send you your license key.

How we use your email: Your email address is used to send you your license key and purchase confirmation. We do not add you to any mailing list automatically. If you'd like to receive occasional updates about TagMagic or future software, we'll only do so if you explicitly ask to be added.


Contact

If you have questions about this policy or how your information is handled, contact us at tagmagicstudio@outlook.com.


Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time as TagMagic or this website changes. The date at the top of this page will reflect the most recent update.

NoizeMagic Privacy Policy:

NoizeMagic uses your microphone only when you click Record. Recordings are saved on your own computer, in your Documents folder, and are never uploaded or sent anywhere. NoizeMagic does not collect personal information, does not require an account, and does not connect to the internet

MirrorMagic Privacy Policy:

MirrorMagic is made by TagMagic Studio. This policy explains what information MirrorMagic and TagMagic Studio handle, and what we do with it. The short version: MirrorMagic runs on your own PC and your own Wi-Fi, has no accounts, and does not send us anything about you or your screen.


1. What MirrorMagic does on your devices

MirrorMagic shows your PC's screen on your tablet and lets you control the PC from the tablet. This happens directly between your PC and your tablet over your local network.

- The picture of your screen, and your taps, scrolling, and typing, travel only between your PC and your tablet. They are never sent to TagMagic Studio or to any other company.
- MirrorMagic does not connect to the internet to work. It has no user accounts and no sign-in.
- MirrorMagic contains no advertising, no analytics, and no tracking.
- MirrorMagic does not record or save your screen.


2. Information MirrorMagic stores on your own devices

MirrorMagic keeps a small amount of information on your devices so that it can work. This information stays on your devices and is not sent to us.

On your PC:
- The date your free trial started, and the date MirrorMagic was last used. This is how the 7-day trial is counted.
- If you unlock MirrorMagic, the email address and license key you typed in.
These are kept in a small file in your Windows user profile. Uninstalling MirrorMagic does not remove this file. You can delete it yourself from the MirrorMagic folder inside your AppData folder.

On your tablet:
- Any "saved views" you create. These are kept in your tablet's web browser. You can remove them inside MirrorMagic, or by clearing the browser's site data.

In Windows:
- The installer adds one Windows Firewall rule so that your tablet can reach MirrorMagic on your local network. Uninstalling MirrorMagic removes it.


3. Information we receive when you buy a license

Licenses are sold through Stripe, a payment company. When you buy:

- Stripe collects your payment details. We never see or store your full card number.
- Stripe gives us your email address, your name if you provided it, and details of the purchase such as the date and amount.

We use this information to create your license key, to email the key to you, to keep a record of your purchase, and to help you if you contact support, for example if you lose your key. Your license key is created from your email address.

Stripe handles your payment information under its own privacy policy, which you can read at https://stripe.com/privacy


4. Information we receive when you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write in your message. We use it to reply to you and to help with your request.
