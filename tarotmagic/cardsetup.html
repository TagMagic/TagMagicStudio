TarotMagic · Card Setup

Puts your downloaded 1909 card pictures into the app with the right names.

Step 1 — Choose your card pictures

Click the button, go to the folder where you saved the 78 downloaded card pictures, press Ctrl + A to select them all, then click Open.

Step 2 — Check the cards

Each card below should show the right picture. A green border means it is matched. If a card is empty or wrong, click it and pick the right picture for it.

Step 3 — Save them into TarotMagic

Click Save Cards. When the folder window opens, choose your TarotMagic folder (the one with index.html in it) and click Select Folder, then Save changes / Allow. A cards folder will be made inside it with all 78 pictures.
