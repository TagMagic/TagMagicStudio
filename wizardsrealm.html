BioRhythmMagic

Bring back the classic biorhythm charts of the ’70s, now in glowing color

Biorhythm theory says three natural cycles begin on the day you’re born and rise and fall throughout your life: the Physical cycle (23 days) for energy and stamina, the Emotional cycle (28 days) for mood and creativity, and the Intellectual cycle (33 days) for memory and focus

You can also add the Intuitive cycle (38 days) for hunches and gut feelings

Just enter your birthday and your cycles appear as colorful waves on a month chart. Step through any day or week, or scroll sideways through your entire year to plan ahead. Critical days, when a cycle crosses the zero line, are flagged automatically. Tap any cycle for a detailed reading of what your current level means, where you are in the cycle, and when your next peak, low and critical day arrive

For extra fun, pick from a list of famous people to see how well your rhythms match theirs, or check your Top 5 celebrity matches. You can also compare with a friend, partner or family member. Their cycles show as dashed lines right on your chart
