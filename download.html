FEATURES

Perfect Metadata

Messy song titles. Missing artist names. Wrong track numbers or album names. Select the songs you want checked, and TagMagic looks up what each one actually is — song title, artist, album, track number — and shows you exactly what it found. Nothing changes automatically; you review the results and choose what to save

Total Visibility • Zero Guesswork

Before anything changes, TagMagic shows you every song in a clean, easy-to-read table — like a simple spreadsheet — with all the details laid out side by side. If there's more than one possible match for a song, you'll see every option, not just one guess picked for you. You always know exactly what's about to happen before it happens

Total Control

Nothing is changed or saved to your actual music files until you say so. Look over every fix first, pick the right album art or artist art when there's a choice, and only then approve the changes. And it's not just your music that stays untouched — TagMagic itself is one small program with no installer and no background services, so your PC stays exactly as it was too
