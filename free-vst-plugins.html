FREE VST PLUGINS

TagMagic Studio Presents...

NoizeMagic... Record any sound and play it like an instrument. NoizeMagic is a free sampler for Windows that turns a clap, a hum, a spoken word, or any noise into notes you can play on your keys or pads, and it can change the voice of that sound in seconds

NoizeMagicFX... Record your words and make any instrument say them. NoizeMagicFX is a free vocoder effect for Windows. Put it on an instrument's track, record a phrase, and that instrument speaks your words at the notes you play

FEATURES

NoizeMagic..
Record It & Play It

Click Record, make a sound, and click Stop. NoizeMagic trims the silence, sets the volume, and spreads your sound across the keyboard, ready to play. You can also load any sound file you already have

NoizeMagic..
Change the Voice

Choose from 13 voices, including Deeper, Higher, Giant, Child, Chipmunk, Robot, Whisper, Monster, Male to Female, and Female to Male. Pitch and Size sliders let you fine-tune any of them, and your original recording is never altered

NoizeMagic..
Shape It & Keep It

Trim the start and end, and control how each note fades in and out. Every recording is saved automatically, can be given a name, and is one click away in your Recordings list

NoizeMagicFX..
Works With Any Instrument

Add NoizeMagicFX to the track of any instrument you own. Synths, organs, strings, and brass speak most clearly, and the words follow whatever notes and chords you play

NoizeMagicFX..
No Microphone Routing 

Most vocoders need a live microphone wired into your DAW. NoizeMagicFX works from a phrase you record once inside the plugin, so there is nothing to set up. Record it, then play it as often as you like

NoizeMagicFX..
Hear It Before You Play

The Preview button speaks your phrase through a built-in test chord. Clarity, Mix, and Volume controls let you dial in how clear and how strong the effect is, and Loop repeats the phrase for as long as you hold the notes
